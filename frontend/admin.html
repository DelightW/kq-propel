Staff accounts

Each person signs in as themselves, so the access log shows who did what. Roles: Administrator (everything), Customer service agent (passengers waiting, name corrections, payments) and Analyst (quality metrics only; no passenger records). A new account gets a one-time password, shown once, that must be changed at first sign-in.
