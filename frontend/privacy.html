Privacy Notice

KQ-Propel aviation support assistant

Draft for review. This notice describes what the assistant does with your information as it is built today. It must be reviewed and approved by Kenya Airways, as the data controller, before the assistant is offered to the public.

Who is responsible for your information

Kenya Airways is the data controller for information you give this assistant. Questions about your data, or requests to see, correct or delete it, can be sent to the Kenya Airways Data Protection Officer.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy it is used
Your messages and the assistant's repliesTo answer your question, to let a member of staff continue the conversation if you ask for a person, and to check the quality of answers.
An estimate of whether a message sounds frustratedTo acknowledge a difficult situation and offer you a person. It never changes the policy answer you receive.
Your last question and any flight number, for 30 minutes So that a follow-up such as "what about Business class?" makes sense.
Your M-Pesa phone number, if you choose to pay by M-PesaTo send the payment request to your phone and match the result to your payment.
Your IP address and a session cookieTo keep your conversation private to your browser and to protect the service from misuse.

Who else receives information

  • Safaricom receives your phone number and the amount when you pay by M-Pesa.
  • PayPal handles card payments on its own checkout page; the assistant never sees your card details.
  • The flight data provider receives only the flight number you ask about.
  • Kenya Airways support staff see the conversation if you ask for a person. Phone numbers are shown to them with most digits hidden.
  • If Kenya Airways turns on a cloud language model, your question and the policy text used to answer it are sent to that model's provider, which may be outside Kenya. This will be stated here before it happens.

How long it is kept

Automatic deletion is not yet switched on in this prototype; the periods below will apply once Kenya Airways has approved them. You can delete your conversation yourself at any time.

  • Conversations, handover cases and your IP address are deleted 30 days after your last message.
  • Payment records are kept as financial records, but the phone number is removed after 90 days.
  • The record of which staff member looked at passenger data is kept for 365 days.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to see the information held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. You can delete your conversation at any time with Delete my conversation under the message box. If you no longer have the chat open, contact the address above with the phone number you used or your case reference (for example H-00012).

If you are not satisfied with how your request is handled, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
