Synthetic In-Chat Payment Flow (Prototype Feature, Not Kenya Airways Policy)

Section 1: Status of This Document
The flow described here is a synthetic prototype feature built for KQ-Propel. Kenya Airways
does not offer payment inside a chat assistant, and the steps below do not describe any live
airline service. Verified payment information is held in the payment methods document.

Section 2: Illustrative Prompt and Push
In this prototype flow the assistant reads the booking reference, confirms the amount due, asks
the guest to confirm the mobile number on file, and then triggers a push prompt to that
handset. The guest enters their mobile money PIN on the handset rather than in the chat window.

Section 3: Illustrative Card Checkout
A mobile money push can only reach a subscriber of a local mobile money network, so the
prototype also offers a card route for guests outside those markets. The assistant creates a
hosted checkout with a payment provider and returns the link; the guest enters their card
details on the provider's page, never in the chat window. The card route charges the fee in the
currency the fee is published in, so no currency conversion is applied by the assistant. That
card payment is accepted at all is verified airline information and is recorded in the payment
methods document; the in-chat checkout described here is the synthetic part.

Section 4: Illustrative Confirmation and Timeout
A confirmation message is expected within ninety seconds. If no confirmation arrives the
prototype marks the attempt as timed out and offers the guest a single retry before handing the
booking back to the standard online payment page.

Section 5: Data Handling in the Prototype
No PIN, card number or mobile money credential is ever entered into or stored by the assistant.
The prototype holds only the booking reference, the amount, the chosen payment route and the
outcome status of the attempt.
