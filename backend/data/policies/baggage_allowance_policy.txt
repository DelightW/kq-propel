Kenya Airways Checked Baggage Allowance

Section 1: Free Checked Allowance by Cabin and Route
Kenya Airways applies the piece concept to checked baggage. On journeys between Africa and
Europe or the Americas, Economy guests may check two pieces of up to 23kg (50lb) each, and
Business guests may check two pieces of up to 32kg (70lb) each. The same two-piece allowance
applies between Africa and the Middle East or Asia, and on flights within Africa.

Section 2: Routes With a One-Piece Allowance
Three routings carry a reduced allowance of a single checked piece. On Bangkok to Guangzhou,
on Nairobi to Mumbai, and on domestic Kenyan flights, Economy guests may check one piece of up
to 23kg and Business guests one piece of up to 32kg. The domestic allowance table covers
services to Kisumu, Mombasa and Malindi.

Section 3: Additional Piece for Asante Members
Members of the Asante Rewards programme at Silver, Gold or Platinum tier receive one extra
checked piece on every route. The additional piece is limited to 23kg for Economy travel and
32kg for Business travel, matching the weight of the member's standard allowance.

Section 4: Weight and Size Limits per Piece
Individual checked pieces are handled to a maximum of 23kg and a maximum of 158cm (62in) when
length, width and height are added together. Any single item weighing more than 32kg, or
measuring more than 300cm in total dimensions, must be authorised in advance by the commercial
team and is recorded in the booking as XBAG.

Section 5: Infants, Strollers and Mobility Equipment
An infant travelling without a seat is carried with one stroller and one car seat free of
charge, in addition to the free cabin allowance. One wheelchair or personal mobility device is
carried free of charge for a guest who requires it, and no weight limit is applied to that
device.

Section 6: Connecting and Interline Journeys
Where a journey involves another carrier, the allowance is determined by the Most Significant
Carrier under IATA Resolution 302. Journeys touching the United States or Canada are assessed
on the piece concept. Guests connecting from a wide-body Boeing 787 onto a narrow-body Boeing
737 or Embraer 170 or 190 should note that carriage of every excess piece on the onward
narrow-body sector cannot be guaranteed.

Section 7: Departures From Jeddah
Guests departing Jeddah may carry one sealed plastic bottle of holy water of up to five litres.
Kenya Airways does not accept liability for this item.
