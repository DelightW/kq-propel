Kenya Airways Payment Methods

Section 1: Mobile Money Acceptance
Kenya Airways accepts mobile money payment for bookings made in Kenya and in Tanzania. The
accepted services are M-Pesa, Airtel Money and Equitel in Kenya, and Vodacom M-Pesa, MTN Mobile
Money and Tigo Pesa in Tanzania.

Section 2: How Mobile Money Payment Is Completed
Mobile money payment is completed inside the online booking flow, which hands off to the
airline's payment gateway. Kenya Airways does not publish a paybill or till number for manual
entry, so a guest cannot pay by initiating a transfer from their handset independently of the
booking.

Section 3: Ticket Issue After Payment
Once payment has been confirmed, the electronic ticket is issued and sent to the guest by
email.

Section 4: Card Payment
Local and international credit and debit cards are accepted. Refunds to a card are returned to
the original card and are processed within 21 to 30 business days.
