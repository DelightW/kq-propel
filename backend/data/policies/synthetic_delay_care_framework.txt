Synthetic Delay Care Framework (Illustrative, Not Kenya Airways Policy)

Section 1: Status of This Document
The figures in this document are synthetic and were written for the KQ-Propel research
prototype. Kenya Airways does not publish monetary compensation amounts for delayed or
cancelled flights, so no published figure was available to use. Nothing here should be quoted
as an airline entitlement. The one published rule in this area is separate and is recorded in
the refunds document: a guest whose flight is delayed by more than eight hours may claim a
refund.

Section 2: Illustrative Delay Bands
Under this illustrative framework a delay of two to four hours attracts a meal voucher, a delay
of four to eight hours attracts a meal voucher and lounge access, and a delay beyond eight
hours attracts hotel accommodation with airport transfer where an overnight stay is required.

Section 3: Illustrative Rebooking Handling
A guest affected by a cancellation would be offered the next available service in the same
cabin at no additional charge, or a refund under the published refund rules. Where the next
available service departs more than twelve hours later, accommodation would be arranged.

Section 4: Why This Document Exists
The prototype needed a policy area where no authoritative source exists, so that the retrieval
and citation behaviour of the agent could be observed on clearly marked synthetic content
alongside verified content. Retrieval results drawn from this document are expected to be
labelled as synthetic in any evaluation.
