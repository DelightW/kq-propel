Kenya Airways Hand Baggage Policy

Section 1: Cabin Allowance by Class
Business guests may bring two cabin pieces plus one accessory, up to a combined 18kg (39lb), on
every route. Economy guests may bring one cabin piece plus one accessory. The Economy weight
limit is 12kg (26lb) on flights to and from the European Union, the United States and the
United Kingdom, and 10kg (22lb) on all other routes.

Section 2: Cabin Baggage Dimensions
Each cabin piece must not exceed 55cm in length, 25cm in width and 35cm in height. The
permitted accessory, which may be a handbag, backpack, briefcase, camera bag or laptop bag,
must not exceed 40cm by 15cm by 30cm.

Section 3: Duty Free Purchases
One bag of duty free purchases is carried in addition to the free cabin allowance, for each
guest.

Section 4: Infants and Children in the Cabin
An infant under two years of age who does not occupy a seat is allowed one cabin piece of up to
12kg within the standard 55cm by 25cm by 35cm dimensions. Where an infant or small child
occupies a seat, one car seat is carried in addition to the normal cabin allowance.
