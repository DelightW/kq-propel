Kenya Airways Excess and Heavy Baggage Fees

Section 1: How Excess Baggage Is Charged
Kenya Airways charges a flat heavy bag fee per additional or overweight piece, set by region.
The airline does not publish a per-kilogram tariff or weight-band table, so the charge depends
on the region of travel and on whether the journey begins at the Nairobi hub rather than on how
many kilograms the bag is over.

Section 2: Heavy Bag Fees by Region
Journeys beginning at the Nairobi hub are charged 75 for Europe, 75 for the Americas, 50 for
Asia, 50 for the Middle East, 50 within Africa and 25 on domestic services. Journeys beginning
at all other points are charged 100 for Europe, 100 for the Americas, 100 for Asia, 75 for the
Middle East, 60 within Africa and 25 on domestic services. Fifth freedom sectors are charged 50.
Kenya Airways publishes this fee table without a currency label; the administrative fees listed
on the same page are stated in US dollars.

Section 3: Firearms and Ammunition
Sporting firearms and ammunition attract a separate handling charge of 25 on domestic routings
and 50 from all other points. As with the heavy bag table, the published figures carry no
currency label.

Section 4: Items Requiring Prior Authorisation
A single item weighing more than 32kg, or exceeding 300cm when length, width and height are
added together, cannot be accepted at the counter without prior approval from the commercial
team. Approved items are flagged in the booking record as XBAG.

Section 5: Booking and Change Service Fees
Booking a ticket by telephone costs from 50 US dollars, rising to 100 US dollars for Business
class. Changing or cancelling a booking, whether online through Manage My Booking or through an
agent, costs between 100 and 300 US dollars depending on the fare. Booking at an airport ticket
office costs between 10 and 100 US dollars, and issuing an award ticket by telephone costs 20
US dollars.
