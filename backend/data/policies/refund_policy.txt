KQ-Propel Refund and Ticket Change Policy

Section 1: Voluntary Refunds
Fully refundable tickets may be cancelled at any time prior to departure for a full refund, less any
applicable service fee of Ksh 1,000. Non-refundable tickets are eligible for a partial credit voucher
valid for 12 months from the date of issue, less a Ksh 2,500 change fee.

Section 2: Involuntary Refunds
Where a flight is cancelled, significantly delayed, or rescheduled by the airline, passengers are
entitled to a full refund with no service fee deducted, regardless of the original fare type.

Section 3: Refund Processing Timelines
Refunds to credit or debit cards are processed within 7 to 14 business days. Refunds to M-Pesa mobile
money accounts, where applicable, are processed within 3 to 5 business days via the Safaricom Daraja
payment gateway.

Section 4: Ticket Date and Route Changes
Passengers may change their travel date or route up to 3 hours before scheduled departure, subject to
fare difference and a rebooking fee of Ksh 2,000 for economy class and Ksh 1,000 for business class.

Section 5: Name Corrections
Minor name corrections of up to 3 characters are permitted free of charge. Full name changes are treated
as a new booking and are not permitted under this policy.
