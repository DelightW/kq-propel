Kenya Airways Refunds and Ticket Changes

Section 1: How Refunds Are Paid
A refund is returned to the original form of payment only. Refunds on tickets bought with a
credit or debit card are processed within 21 to 30 business days. That period does not include
the time the guest's own bank takes to post the funds.

Section 2: Non-refundable Tickets
A non-refundable ticket may be rescheduled within one year of the date the original ticket was
issued, less any applicable change fees. Applicable taxes, charges and fees remain refundable
even on a non-refundable ticket where the guest does not travel.

Section 3: Refunds After a Long Delay
A guest who cancelled because their flight was delayed by more than eight hours may claim a
refund using the online refund form. This route applies to tickets purchased on
kenya-airways.com.

Section 4: Change and Cancellation Fees
Changing or cancelling a booking costs between 100 and 300 US dollars, whether the change is
made online through Manage My Booking, by telephone or through an agent.

Section 5: Name Corrections and Name Changes
Correcting up to three characters of a name costs 15 US dollars on a domestic booking and 75
US dollars on an international booking. A full name change costs 50 US dollars on a domestic
booking and 150 US dollars on an international booking. Name changes apply to Kenya Airways
operated flights only and are not available on interline itineraries.

Section 6: Bereavement and Medical Cases
Where a guest has died, a full refund is available for that guest only. Accompanying family
members are not refunded, but the date change fee is waived. Medical circumstances do not
create an automatic right to a refund or a free change, and the rules of the fare continue to
apply.

Section 7: Tickets Bought Through an Agency
Kenya Airways cannot refund a ticket issued by a travel agency. The guest must approach the
agency that issued the ticket.
