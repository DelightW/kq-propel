KQ-Propel Flight Delay and Cancellation Compensation Policy

Section 1: Delay Notification
Passengers will be notified of any delay exceeding 30 minutes via SMS, email, and the KQ-Propel
digital assistant. Real-time flight status, including gate changes and estimated departure time, is
sourced live from the AviationStack flight telemetry service.

Section 2: Compensation for Short Delays (1-3 hours)
Passengers delayed between 1 and 3 hours are entitled to a meal voucher of Ksh 1,500 and complimentary
access to the airport lounge where available.

Section 3: Compensation for Extended Delays (3-6 hours)
Passengers delayed between 3 and 6 hours are entitled to a meal voucher of Ksh 1,500, complimentary
lounge access, and a rebooking option onto the next available flight at no additional cost.

Section 4: Compensation for Overnight Delays (over 6 hours)
Passengers delayed more than 6 hours, or whose flight is rescheduled to the following day, are entitled
to a hotel accommodation voucher, ground transport to and from the hotel, and meal vouchers for each
affected meal period.

Section 5: Flight Cancellation
In the event of a flight cancellation, passengers may choose between a full refund processed to the
original payment method within 14 business days, or free rebooking on the next available flight. Refund
requests can be initiated directly through the KQ-Propel digital assistant.

Section 6: Force Majeure Exclusions
Compensation under this policy does not apply to delays or cancellations caused by extreme weather,
air traffic control restrictions, security threats, or airport authority directives, in line with
force majeure provisions.
