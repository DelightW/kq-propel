KQ-Propel Airline Baggage Policy Manual

Section 1: Checked Baggage Allowance
Economy class passengers are permitted one (1) checked bag not exceeding 23kg. Business class
passengers are permitted two (2) checked bags, each not exceeding 32kg. Infants travelling without
a seat are permitted one (1) checked bag not exceeding 10kg.

Section 2: Overweight Baggage Fees
Any checked bag exceeding the permitted weight allowance is classified as overweight baggage and is
subject to a supplementary fee. Bags between 1kg and 10kg over the limit incur a flat fee of Ksh 5,000.
Bags between 11kg and 20kg over the limit incur a flat fee of Ksh 9,000. Bags exceeding 20kg over the
limit will not be accepted for standard check-in and must be shipped as cargo.

Section 3: Payment of Overweight Fees
Overweight baggage fees may be settled at the airport counter using cash, card, or M-Pesa till payment,
or in advance through the KQ-Propel digital assistant using a Safaricom M-Pesa STK push initiated
directly within the chat interface. A digital receipt and reference code are issued immediately upon
successful payment confirmation from the Daraja payment gateway.

Section 4: Lost or Delayed Baggage
Passengers whose baggage is delayed by more than 24 hours are entitled to file a Property Irregularity
Report (PIR) and may claim a daily incidental allowance of Ksh 2,000 for up to 5 days while the baggage
is traced. Claims must be submitted within 7 days of the incident.

Section 5: Special and Sporting Equipment
Sporting equipment such as golf bags, bicycles, and skis are accepted as checked baggage subject to a
special handling fee of Ksh 3,500 per item, regardless of the standard weight allowance.
