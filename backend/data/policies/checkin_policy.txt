Kenya Airways Check-in and Baggage Drop-off

Section 1: Online and Mobile Check-in
Online and mobile check-in opens 48 hours before departure on international services. On
flights involving the United States, check-in opens 24 hours before departure. Online check-in
closes one hour before departure on all services.

Section 2: International Airport Check-in
Guests travelling internationally must complete airport check-in at least two and a half hours
before departure, in every class of travel. International check-in counters close one hour
before departure.

Section 3: Domestic Airport Check-in
Guests travelling on domestic services must complete airport check-in at least one and a half
hours before departure, in every class of travel. Domestic check-in counters close 30 minutes
before departure.

Section 4: Departures From Guangzhou
Check-in counters for departures from Guangzhou, China close 90 minutes before departure.

Section 5: Baggage Drop-off
On domestic services, baggage may be dropped off up to one hour before the flight's closing
time. On international services, a minimum of three hours is required for baggage drop-off.
