KQ-Propel Check-in and Boarding Policy

Section 1: Online and Mobile Check-in
Online and mobile check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 90 minutes before departure for
international flights, and 60 minutes before departure for domestic flights.

Section 2: Airport Check-in Counters
Airport check-in counters open 3 hours before departure for international flights and 2 hours before
departure for domestic flights. Counters close 60 minutes before international departure and 45 minutes
before domestic departure.

Section 3: Boarding Gate Timelines
Boarding gates open 45 minutes before scheduled departure and close 15 minutes before departure.
Passengers who arrive at the gate after closure will be declared a no-show and may be subject to the
no-show policy under the fare rules of their ticket.

Section 4: Travel Documentation
Passengers must present a valid passport with at least 6 months validity beyond the date of travel, and
any required visa or transit documentation, at check-in. The KQ-Propel digital assistant can advise on
destination-specific entry requirements based on the passenger's nationality.

Section 5: Special Assistance
Passengers requiring wheelchair assistance, unaccompanied minor services, or medical clearance must
notify the airline at least 48 hours before departure through the digital assistant or a call center
agent.
