Kenya Airways Delayed and Lost Baggage

Section 1: Reporting Baggage That Has Not Arrived
A guest whose baggage does not arrive should report it before leaving the airport so that a
file is created and a file reference number is issued. That reference number is required for
all tracing and for any later claim. Tracing can be followed online through the WorldTracer
service at worldtracer.aero/filedsp/kq.htm, and the baggage tracking line is +254-20-3274747.
Kenya Airways refers to this record as a file creation and a file reference number rather than
as a Property Irregularity Report.

Section 2: First Needs Allowance
Where baggage has not been received within 24 hours of the file being created, a First Needs
Allowance may be claimed towards essential items. The allowance is limited to 150 US dollars
for First Class and Premier World guests and 100 US dollars for Economy guests. Children aged
between two and twelve receive 50 per cent of the applicable limit in Economy and Business.
Children under two years of age are not entitled to the allowance.

Section 3: Conditions Attached to the Allowance
Receipts are required and the allowance covers essential items only. A guest who is resident in
the country of arrival is not eligible unless they can show proof of residence in another
country for the previous six months. Where a guest has received at least one of their bags,
entitlement may not arise. Any First Needs Allowance paid is deducted from the final
compensation if the baggage is ultimately declared lost.

Section 4: Deadline for Compensation Claims
A claim for compensation must be submitted in writing to the nearest Kenya Airways airport
office within seven days of arrival.

Section 5: Limits on Liability
Liability may be reduced where baggage was unsuitably packed for air travel, or where it was
presented for check-in later than the recommended time.
