Kenya Airways Sporting and Special Baggage

Section 1: How Sporting Equipment Is Treated
Sporting equipment is treated as a standard checked piece and is carried within the free
allowance unless that allowance is exceeded. Charges below apply only once the guest's free
allowance has been used. All published rates carry no currency label on the Kenya Airways fee
table.

Section 2: Rates for Common Sporting Equipment
Golf equipment, bicycles, diving equipment, surfboards, fishing equipment, skis and snowboards
are charged 110 between Africa and Europe or the Americas, 90 between Africa and the Middle
East or Asia, 50 within Africa and 40 on domestic services.

Section 3: Rates for Bulky Sporting Equipment
Large fishing equipment, water ski equipment and general sports equipment are charged 120
between Africa and Europe or the Americas, 100 between Africa and the Middle East or Asia, 60
within Africa and 50 on domestic services. Charges for golf equipment are doubled where the
booking is a round trip.

Section 4: Size and Weight Conditions
Sporting equipment must not exceed 300cm when length, width and height are added together, and
the maximum weight for the guest's class of travel continues to apply. A ski bag may not exceed
300cm (118in) in length, and a pair of ski bags must together stay within the 23kg or 32kg
limit that applies to the guest's allowance.

Section 5: Diving Equipment
A special excess charge covers one set of diving equipment weighing up to 20kg for a one way
journey. A scuba tank is not classified as diving equipment for this purpose.

Section 6: Bicycles and Aircraft Restrictions
A maximum of three bicycles may be carried on Embraer 170 and 190 aircraft. Electric bicycles
are not accepted for carriage.

Section 7: Mobility Devices
No weight limitation is applied to a wheelchair or personal mobility device.
